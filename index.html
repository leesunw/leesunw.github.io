About Me

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Linguistics at Georgetown University. My research examines bilingual speech, L2 pronunciation, and how accented speech is evaluated across different social, linguistic, and technological contexts.

I am especially interested in how beliefs about pronunciation shape human judgments, and how these evaluations compare with AI-based assessment.
